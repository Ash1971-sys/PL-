How to use the board
  1. Pick a setup. Drag a numbered piece from the bench onto the pitch to add it.
  2. Drag a player to shape the team. Select a piece to edit their number or send them to the bench.
  3. Pick Draw run or Draw pass and drag an arrow, or tap its start and end.
  4. Use Present board to show the pitch and arrows to the team.
